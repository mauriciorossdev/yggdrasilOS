# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome newcomers, accept constructive criticism gracefully, and focus on what is best for the
community. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository's private reporting channel (GitHub *Security → Report a
vulnerability*, or a direct message to a maintainer). Maintainers will review and respond to every report, and may
remove content or restrict participation to keep the community safe.

This text is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
